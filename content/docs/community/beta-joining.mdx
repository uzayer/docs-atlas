Joining takes a few minutes of your time. The rest is us reviewing your sign-up and adding you to the organization.

## Sign up

1. **Install Atlas.** Follow [Install](/docs/getting-started/install) for macOS, Windows or Linux.
2. **Create an Atlas account.** Sign in from the desktop app as described in [Your account](/docs/team/accounts#sign-in-from-the-desktop-app). Being added to an organization needs an account.
3. **Sign up at [credits.tryatlas.cc](https://credits.tryatlas.cc).** Choose the beta tester option and submit. Use the email address you sign in to Atlas with, so we can find your account.
4. **Accept the invitation.** When we add you, you get an email invitation to atlas-beta. Its link opens the web app, where you accept it. See [Join with an invite link](/docs/team/web-app#join-with-an-invite-link).
5. **Open the organization in the desktop app.** Open the organization switcher at the top of the rail and select the refresh button to pull in organizations you were added to elsewhere. Then select **atlas-beta**. See [Switch organizations](/docs/team/organizations#switch-organizations).

The Atlas team reviews every sign-up and decides who joins each round, so being added isn't instant. If you've heard nothing after a while, ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/GmnFggaPfP).

## Keep your own work in your own organization

Everyone in atlas-beta can read every session in its cloud projects. So don't put a private codebase's sessions there.

Run Atlas on your own projects in your own organization, the way you normally would. When you need to show us a session, share that one session (see [Attach the session](/docs/community/beta-testing#attach-the-session)). To test the team features with your own team, create a synced organization and invite them; see [Organizations](/docs/team/organizations).

## Your first week

- [ ] **Say hello** in `#introductions`: what you build, your team size, and which agents you use.
- [ ] **Read the current focus areas** in `#announcements`.
- [ ] **Connect your agents.** Add the agents you already use from the [registry](/docs/agents/registry), and try [Atlas Agent](/docs/agents/atlas-agent).
- [ ] **Record a session.** Turn on [session capture](/docs/source-control/capture) in a real project, do some work with an agent, commit it, and find the session on the [timeline](/docs/team/timeline).
- [ ] **Try the team features with us.** Post in a channel, join a call, or co-write a [prompt draft](/docs/team/prompt-drafts) in atlas-beta.
- [ ] **Send your first report.** A bug, something confusing, or something you liked. One line is enough. [Testing Atlas](/docs/community/beta-testing#report-a-bug) shows what a good report has in it.
- [ ] **Send feedback from the app** once, so you know where it lives: select the **?** at the bottom of the rail, then **Send feedback**.
- [ ] **Want to write code?** Look at [Contributing](/docs/community/contributing) and the open `good first issue` list.

## Leaving

You can leave at any time from the organization settings in the web app; see [Leave an organization](/docs/team/members#leave-an-organization). Your account and your own organizations are not affected.
