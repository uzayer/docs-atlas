Joining takes a few minutes of your time. The rest is us adding you to the organisation, which we do by hand.

## Sign up

1. **Install Atlas.** Follow [Install](/docs/getting-started/install) for macOS, Windows or Linux.
2. **Create an Atlas account.** Sign in from the desktop app as described in [Your account](/docs/team/accounts#sign-in-from-the-desktop-app). Being added to an organisation needs an account.
3. **Sign up at [credits.tryatlas.cc](https://credits.tryatlas.cc).** Use the email address you sign in to Atlas with, so we can find your account. {/* TODO(founders): name the exact option or field on credits.tryatlas.cc that marks a sign-up as a beta tester. */}
4. **Accept the invitation.** When we add you, you get an email invitation to Atlas Contributors. Its link opens the web app, where you accept it. See [Join with an invite link](/docs/team/web-app#join-with-an-invite-link).
5. **Open the organisation in the desktop app.** Open the organisation switcher at the top of the rail and select the refresh button to pull in organisations you were added to elsewhere. Then select **Atlas Contributors**. See [Switch organisations](/docs/team/organisations#switch-organisations).

We review sign-ups by hand, so being added isn't instant. If you've heard nothing after a while, ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/GmnFggaPfP). {/* TODO(founders): say who reviews sign-ups and what, if anything, decides whether someone is added. */}

## Keep your own work in your own organisation

Everyone in Atlas Contributors can read every session in its cloud projects. So don't put a private codebase's sessions there.

Run Atlas on your own projects in your own organisation, the way you normally would. When you need to show us a session, share that one session (see [Attach the session](/docs/community/beta-testing#attach-the-session)). To test the team features with your own team, create a synced organisation and invite them; see [Organisations](/docs/team/organisations).

## Your first week

- [ ] **Say hello** in the introductions channel: what you build, your team size, and which agents you use. {/* TODO(founders): confirm the channel name (proposed: #introductions). */}
- [ ] **Read the current focus areas** in the announcements channel and on [Testing Atlas](/docs/community/beta-testing#what-to-test). {/* TODO(founders): confirm the channel name (proposed: #announcements). */}
- [ ] **Connect your agents.** Add the agents you already use from the [registry](/docs/agents/registry), and try [Atlas Agent](/docs/agents/atlas-agent).
- [ ] **Record a session.** Turn on [session capture](/docs/source-control/capture) in a real project, do some work with an agent, commit it, and find the session on the [timeline](/docs/team/timeline).
- [ ] **Try the team features with us.** Post in a channel, join a call, or co-write a [prompt draft](/docs/team/prompt-drafts) in Atlas Contributors.
- [ ] **Send your first report.** A bug, something confusing, or something you liked. One line is enough. [Testing Atlas](/docs/community/beta-testing#report-a-bug) shows what a good report has in it.
- [ ] **Send feedback from the app** once, so you know where it lives: select the **?** at the bottom of the rail, then **Send feedback**.
- [ ] **Want to write code?** Look at [Contributing](/docs/community/contributing) and the open `good first issue` list.

## Leaving

You can leave at any time from the organisation settings in the web app; see [Leave an organisation](/docs/team/members#leave-an-organisation). Your account and your own organisations are not affected.
