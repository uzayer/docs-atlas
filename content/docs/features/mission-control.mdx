Mission Control is a dashboard covering every project you have open in Atlas, rather than the one you're currently in. It answers where your agent time and money actually went.

![The Mission Control dashboard with usage charts across projects](/screenshots/placeholder.svg)

## What it shows

Pick a window (7, 30, or 90 days, or everything) and the dashboard reports across it:

- **Totals**: tokens, requests, sessions, and estimated cost.
- **Usage over time**, so a spike has a date attached to it.
- **Consumption by project**, as a share of the whole.
- **A timeline per project**, showing when each was active.
- **A log table** underneath the charts, for reading the detail behind a number.

Token counts are broken out by kind where the provider reports them, including cache reads and cache writes, which is usually where an unexpected bill comes from.

<Callout type="info">
  Coverage is per-agent, not universal. Mission Control reports usage for Claude Code, Codex, and [Review](/docs/features/review) runs: those are the sources that expose the numbers it needs. Other agents' sessions are recorded in your [timeline](/docs/features/timeline) but don't appear in these totals.
</Callout>

## Exporting

The dashboard exports as PDF, as an image, or as markdown.
