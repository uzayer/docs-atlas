Manage people in a cloud [organisation](/docs/organisation/organisations) while signed in. Open the organisation switcher and select **Invite & Manage members**.

Local organisations cannot have members. Sign in or turn on sync first when Atlas asks you to.

## Invite a teammate

Admins can add one or more email addresses, choose a role, and select **Invite**. Atlas sends each invitation separately, so one failure does not block the rest.

<Callout type="warn">
  Atlas does not send invitation emails yet. It copies each acceptance link to your clipboard. Send that link to your teammate, and cancel then recreate the invitation if you lose it.
</Callout>

Use the **Invitations** tab to see or cancel outstanding invites. The **Members** tab shows every joined member, their role, and when they joined.

## Choose a role

Atlas has four roles: **Admin**, **Product Owner**, **Developer**, and **Member**. The person who creates an organisation is its admin.

Only Admin currently changes desktop-app permissions. Admins can invite people, change roles, cancel invites, remove members, see invitations, and delete the organisation server-side. The other three roles can open the organisation and view the roster.

Change a role from the member row's **⋯** menu. The update is immediate for you; the other person receives it when their client next refreshes its token.

## Remove a member

Admins can remove another member from the **⋯** menu. To remove yourself, choose **Leave organisation**.

<Callout type="warn">
  An admin must give another person the Admin role before leaving. This prevents an organisation from losing its last administrator.
</Callout>
