Everything else in Atlas works without an account. This section is about the part that doesn't: your Atlas account, the organisations it belongs to, and who else is in them.

It's worth being precise about what that gets you today, because the name suggests more than it does.

<Callout type="info">
  **Organisations govern accounts and access, not shared work.** Signing in and inviting a teammate does not make their sessions, chats, or agent history appear on your machine.

  Atlas is single-player today: your [sessions](/docs/getting-started/concepts#session) and [memory](/docs/context/memory) stay on your device, and the thing you already share with your team is the git repository.
</Callout>

## How the pieces fit

An [organisation](/docs/getting-started/concepts#organisation) owns [projects](/docs/getting-started/concepts#project). Exactly one is active per window, and the switcher at the top of the sidebar is where you move between them.

An organisation can be **local** or **cloud**. A local one exists only on your machine and needs no account at all.

A cloud one is created in your Atlas account, which is what gives it a handle, members, and roles.

Your account is separate from any organisation. You sign in once, and that identity is what unlocks the cloud organisations you belong to.
