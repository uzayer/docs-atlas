Research is a tab for searching academic literature and pulling a paper into the project, so an agent can work from the actual source rather than from your summary of it.

## Searching

Search runs against **arXiv** or **Semantic Scholar**, switchable per search. Results show the title, authors, abstract, publication date, and subject categories, with a link out to the paper and to its PDF.

## Bringing a paper in

Download a paper and it's saved into the project, where it becomes available as context. Papers are one of the categories the `@` [mention](/docs/getting-started/concepts#mention) picker searches, so referencing one in the [composer](/docs/product/chat) is the same gesture as referencing a file.
