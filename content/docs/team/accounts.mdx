You can use Atlas without an account. Chat, the editor, memory, and the timeline all work locally. Sign in when you want to join or create cloud [organisations](/docs/team/organisations).

## Sign in from Atlas

Select the account control in the title bar. Atlas opens an approval page in your browser and shows an eight-character code.

1. Enter the code on the approval page.
2. Select **Approve**.
3. Return to Atlas when the dialog closes.

The code confirms that you are approving the session on this device. If the browser does not open, copy the approval URL and code from the dialog. Expired or denied codes offer **Try again**.

## Stay signed in safely

Atlas stores the credential on your machine and keeps you signed in across restarts. The interface receives your identity and organisation list, but never your session or access token.

Atlas caches your profile photo locally, so it remains available offline. An outage or rate limit does not sign you out. Only a rejected or expired server session does.

## Sign out

Select **Sign Out** from the account menu. Atlas immediately clears the credential, identity, and cached photo on this device, then requests server-side revocation in the background.

Signing out does not delete your local organisations or remove you from cloud organisations. Sign in again to open cloud organisations.
