We are building an Atlas app for iOS and Android. It is a client for your organisation, like the [web app](/docs/team/web-app): it keeps you in the conversation and lets you read the record of agent work when you are away from your desk. It does not run agents.

It is not in the App Store or Google Play yet.

## What it covers

- **Chat**: your channels, DMs and group DMs, live. Send, edit and delete messages, react, pin, attach files, mention teammates, search, and start new conversations. [Session references](/docs/team/chat#session-references) open the recorded session.
- **Timeline**: the organisation's sessions as they happen, with the full session reader, comments and share links.
- **Inbox**: comments and mentions that need you, and recent activity across your organisations.
- **Projects**: the organisation's projects and their details.
- **Members**: the roster, invitations and roles.
- **Dashboard**: usage and live activity.
- **Settings**: your account and the organisation's settings.

You sign in by approving a code at app.tryatlas.cc/device, the same way the desktop app signs in. There is no password on the phone.

## What it does not cover

- **Calls.** The app shows a live call and, after a meeting, its recording, transcript and call note, but you join calls from the web or desktop app.
- **Push notifications.**
- **Spaces, prompt drafts and shared threads.**
