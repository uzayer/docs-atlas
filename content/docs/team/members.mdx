Members belong to a synced [organisation](/docs/team/organisations). A local organisation has no members; turn on sync first.

Open the members manager from **Members** in the rail, or from **Invite & Manage members** in the organisation switcher. In the [web app](/docs/team/web-app), select **Members** in the sidebar.

The manager has two tabs. **Members** lists everyone who has joined, with their role and when they joined. **Invitations** lists invitations that haven't been accepted yet.

## Roles

Every member has one of four roles. The person who creates the organisation is an Admin.

| Role | What it can do today |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can, plus invite people, change roles, remove members, manage invite links, rename the organisation, choose its AI models, see everyone's usage, set the sharing policy, and delete the organisation. |
| **Product Owner** | The same access as Member. |
| **Developer** | The same access as Member. |
| **Member** | Read the timeline, sync sessions to the cloud projects they can see, comment, and use chat, calls, prompt drafts and Spaces. |

Today, Admin is the only role that changes what someone can do. Product Owner, Developer and Member are labels you can use to describe someone's part in the team.

An admin does not get a key to everybody's conversations. Private channels and direct messages stay readable only by the people in them, admins included.

### The owner

The person who created the organisation is also its **Owner**. The owner:

- can't be removed and can't leave the organisation
- is the only person who can permanently delete recorded sessions, either one session at a time or all of one member's sessions

Ownership can't be transferred. The web app marks the owner with an **Owner** tag in the members list.

## Invite people by email

Only admins can invite.

1. Open the members manager.
2. Type one or more email addresses into the invite bar. A comma, or Enter, turns each address into a chip.
3. Choose a role from the picker beside the addresses.
4. Select **Invite**.

Atlas emails each person an invitation with a link to accept it. The link expires after 48 hours. Atlas sends invitations one at a time, so a bad address doesn't stop the rest. The desktop app also copies the acceptance links to your clipboard, in case you'd rather send them yourself.

On the **Invitations** tab, select **Copy invite link** to copy an invitation's link again, or **Cancel invite** to withdraw it. In the web app, an admin can also **Resend invite**.

## Share an invite link

An invite link lets several people join without inviting each of them by address. Create one in the web app, in the organisation settings under **Invite links**:

1. Select **New link**.
2. Choose what people join as under **Joins as**: Member, Developer or Product Owner. A link never makes anybody an admin.
3. Choose **Uses**: 1, 5, 10, 25 or no limit. Each person who joins uses one.
4. Choose **Expires after**: 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days, or never.
5. Optionally, list domains under **Only these email domains**, so a forwarded link doesn't work for anybody outside your company.
6. Select **Create link**. The link is copied to your clipboard.

Anyone with the link opens it in a browser, signs in, and selects **Join &lt;organisation&gt;**. A link that has expired, been revoked or been used up says so, and asks them to get a new one.

To stop a link working, select **Revoke**. People who already joined with it stay.

## Change a role

Admins change a role from the **⋯** menu on a member's row, under **Role**. In the web app it's **Change role…**.

The change takes effect for you at once. The other person picks it up when their app next refreshes its sign-in, within a few minutes.

## Remove a member

Admins remove a member from the **⋯** menu with **Remove from organisation**. Their recorded sessions stay on the timeline, shown as **Former member**. The owner can delete them if they should go too.

## Leave an organisation

Leave from the web app: open the organisation settings and select **Leave** under **Danger zone**. You lose access until somebody invites you back.

The owner can't leave. In the desktop app, an admin can't leave either until they give someone else the Admin role, so the organisation never loses its last admin.
