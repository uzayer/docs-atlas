Team chat is your organisation's messaging, inside Atlas: channels, direct messages and group DMs. It is where you talk to teammates, and where a recorded agent session can be dropped into the conversation so everyone opens the same record.

Team chat is not the same as [agent chat](/docs/product/chat). Agent chat is you talking to an agent in a project. Team chat is people talking to people across the organisation, and it lives in the right-hand panel next to Source Control.

## Open team chat

Press <Kbd>⌘⇧C</Kbd>, or click **Chat** in the project rail. <Kbd>⌘⇧B</Kbd> gives the panel back to Source Control.

Chat needs a [synced organisation](/docs/team/organisations). In a local-only organisation the **Chat** button tells you to sync the organisation first, because there is no server to talk to.

The same conversations are in the [web app](/docs/team/web-app) at app.tryatlas.cc, and messages arrive live in both.

## Find your way around

The home view lists every channel you are in, then your direct messages, then the rest of your teammates. Type in **Jump to a channel or person…** to filter all three.

Click a channel or a person to open the conversation as a tab at the top of the panel. You can keep several open, for example a channel you are watching and a DM you are answering. Click the back arrow to return to the home view.

Public channels you haven't joined are under **Discover** at the bottom of the home view. Expand it and click **Join**.

## Channels

Click **+** beside **Channels**, type a name, and choose whether it is **Private**.

- **Public** channels are visible to everyone in the organisation. Anyone can be invited, and a new member sees the full history.
- **Private** channels are visible only to their members. An organisation admin cannot read a private channel they are not in.

Members can rename a channel: hover its name in the conversation header and click it.

## Direct messages and group DMs

Click **+** beside **Direct messages** and pick one person for a DM, or several for a group DM. Clicking a teammate on the home view opens your DM with them, creating it if needed.

A group DM's membership is fixed. To add someone, start a new group with everyone in it.

## Write messages

The composer takes Markdown. Its toolbar has bold, italic, strikethrough, code, links and lists, plus an emoji picker.

- Click the paperclip to attach files, up to 10 per message. You can also drop or paste them into the composer.
- Click **@** or type `@` to mention a teammate. A mention notifies them.
- A message holds up to 16 KB of text.

Hover a message for its actions:

- **React** with an emoji.
- **Reply** to quote the message in your next one. Replies stay in the main conversation; there are no separate thread panes.
- **More** to copy the text, **Pin for everyone**, or edit and delete. Only the author can edit a message. The author or an organisation admin can delete it.

## Pins and files

Pins are shared: anything one person pins, everyone sees. When a conversation has pins, a pin count appears in its header. Click it to search the pinned messages and jump to one.

Under the conversation header, the **Files** tab gathers the images, video, audio and documents shared in the conversation. Click an image or video to open it full size.

## Session references

A message can carry a reference to a recorded agent session, or to one [checkpoint](/docs/getting-started/concepts#checkpoint) inside it. It shows as a card with the session's title, agent and changes. Click the card to open that session on the [timeline](/docs/team/timeline), even if a teammate ran it on their machine.

<Screenshot

There are two ways to add one:

- **In the web app**, click **Reference a session** in the composer, find the session, and pick the session or one of its checkpoints. A message carries up to three.
- **From Atlas Agent**, ask it to post about a session, for example "send a summary of this session to #releases". Atlas Agent asks you before it sends anything. This needs [Let Atlas Agent act in your organisation](/docs/agents/atlas-agent) turned on.

References only work for projects visible to the whole organisation. A restricted project's sessions can't be referenced.

## Search

On the desktop, the search box on the home view finds channels and people. To search the text of messages, use **Search messages** in a conversation's header in the web app.

## Notifications

Atlas sends a desktop notification when someone sends you a DM or group DM message, and when someone mentions you in a channel (including `@channel` and `@here`). Click the notification to open the conversation. Turn either kind off under **Notifications** in Settings.

## Post from CI and other tools

An incoming webhook gives a channel a secret URL that outside systems can post to. Use one to have CI report a failed build, a deploy script announce a release, or a monitor raise an alert in the channel where the team will see it.

Webhook messages show live, count as unread, can mention people, appear in search, and are marked as coming from an integration rather than a person.

To create one:

1. Open the channel in the [web app](/docs/team/web-app).
2. Open the conversation menu (**⋯**) and choose **Integrations**.
3. Name the webhook, for example "Deploys", and create it.
4. Copy the URL. It is shown only now and when you regenerate it, so store it as a secret in your CI.

Post JSON with a `content` field (a `text` field also works, so most Slack and Discord integrations work by changing the URL):

```bash
curl -X POST "$ATLAS_WEBHOOK_URL" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"content": "Build #482 failed on main", "username": "CI"}'
```

To attach files, send `multipart/form-data`, up to 10 files and 25 MB in total.

Organisation admins and the person who created the channel manage its webhooks: rename them, give them a picture, regenerate the URL, disable them without deleting, or delete them. Webhooks work in channels only, not in DMs.
