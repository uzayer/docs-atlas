Atlas works on your own, and it works better with a team. Once your [organisation](/docs/team/organisations) is synced, every teammate's recorded sessions land on one shared [timeline](/docs/team/timeline), and the conversation about that work happens next to it: team chat, calls, co-written prompt drafts, Spaces and shared threads.
